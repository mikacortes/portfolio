Background

Furever Diamond is the pet-focused sister brand to Bonded Diamond.

Both brands live under Bonded Diamond LLC and use the same science—custom lab-grown diamonds made from hair or fur—but they speak to different audiences. Bonded Diamond serves human personalization and fine jewelry. Furever Diamond memorializes pets, turning fur into a lasting keepsake.

I designed the Furever Diamond brand identity, packaging, print collateral, and website. For the human-focused side of the business, see my Bonded Diamond case study on Google Ads creative and campaign strategy.

Brand Identity

I defined the visual identity for Furever Diamond.

Logo, typography, color, and brand marks—designed to feel distinct from Bonded Diamond while still living under the same parent company.

Marketing

I carried the brand into packaging and print.

The physical experience had to feel as intentional as the product itself—premium unboxing, clear guidance in welcome materials, and print pieces that carried the brand into customers' hands.

Website Design
