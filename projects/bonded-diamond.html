Background

Bonded Diamond is a luxury lab-grown diamond jewelry brand built around personalization.

The company creates custom diamonds from hair, giving customers a meaningful alternative to traditional fine jewelry. I joined as a Contract Product Designer to support their website, but I was asked to also create marketing materials for Google Ads and help strategize how the brand showed up across different campaigns.

Learning

This project was a strong introduction to Google Ads, and I now feel much more confident using the platform on new campaigns.

Beyond designing the creative itself, I learned how campaign structure, asset sizing, and format-specific layouts shape what actually ships on the platform. Working across multiple campaign themes—from science-forward product storytelling to occasion-based upgrades—helped me think about how design decisions land in a performance marketing context.

Final Mentions

Special thanks to Jason, the CEO of Bonded Diamond!

He reached out to me about working on the project, and I had the chance to collaborate directly with him as founder and CEO. It was especially rewarding to think through business strategy together and translate those priorities into how the ads should look and feel.
