Background

Bonded Diamond is a luxury lab-grown diamond jewelry brand built around personalization.

The company creates custom diamonds from hair, giving customers a meaningful alternative to traditional fine jewelry. I joined as a Product Designer to support their website, but I was asked to also create marketing materials for Google Ads and help strategize how the brand showed up across different campaigns.

I also helped launch Furever Diamond, the pet-focused sister brand—see that case study for brand identity, packaging, print, and website work.
