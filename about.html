About Me

I’m Monica Cortes (she/her), a UC Berkeley graduate (Fall 2024) with degrees in Cognitive Science and Data Science and the Berkeley Certificate in Design Innovation. During university, I discovered my passion for applying design and data science to social good — the same thread that runs through my work with startups and nonprofits as a designer and analyst.

I’m interested in thoughtful systems, visual experiences, and product direction. I’ve supported product teams, marketing efforts, and large-scale publications — from parole-readiness tools and nonprofit websites to accreditation reports and healthcare social media. I’m based in Southern California and open to relocation for full-time roles with positive impact.

Outside of work, I enjoy listening to commentary on popular or niche media, crocheting and knitting, keeping up with Formula 1, and watching as many movies as possible. I’m also building toward full-stack skills and hope to launch an app someday.

How I Work

Work Process

Discovery & Planning

I start by understanding goals, users, and constraints — through stakeholder conversations, competitive research, and whatever data is available. Whether I’m shaping a product flow or a content strategy, I want the problem framed clearly before anything gets designed.

Design & Implementation

I move from research into prototypes, visual systems, and deliverables — often in Figma, InDesign, or the tools a team already uses. I collaborate closely with engineers, executives, and non-designers so the work stays practical and easy to maintain after handoff.

Testing & Refinement

I test early and iterate often: usability sessions, stakeholder feedback, and performance data when it’s available. Small details matter, but so does knowing when something is ready to ship — especially on tight timelines.
